OPTIMIZATION GUIDE
{# Top move — scenario-aware, updated by updateROIGuide() #}
🏆
Best ROI Move: Pump during solar hours — store water, not electricity
Scheduling irrigation between 08:00–16:00 eliminates the need for battery storage entirely. Water tanks cost 5–10× less than batteries and never degrade. This single change can cut payback from 7 to 4 years.
↓ Payback: −3 yrs ↓ CapEx: −35%
{# Move 1 #}
🔋
Reduce battery bank size
Oversized batteries are the #1 payback killer. Right-size to 1.5–2 nights of essential load only.
↓ Payback ↓ CapEx −20%
Neutral — current sizing
{# Move 2 #}
☀️
Increase solar array size
More panels = more free energy during the day. Panels now cost less than batteries per kWh stored.
↑ Generation Moderate CapEx ↑
Neutral — current sizing
{# Move 3 #}
💧
Switch to drip irrigation
Drip reduces water demand by 40–60% vs flood/sprinkler. Less water = smaller pump = less energy = smaller solar.
↓ Payback −2 yrs ↓ CapEx −15%
Drip: pump demand −50%, payback improves
{# Move 4 #}
⛽
Replace diesel run hours
Every hour of diesel replaced by solar saves MWK 400–800. Diesel cost is the fastest ROI lever.
↓ Payback −1 yr/hr
4 hrs replaced → MWK 64K/month savings
{# Move 5 #}
🌬️
Raise wind tower height
Wind speed increases ~10–15% per 10m of tower height. Higher tower = exponentially more energy.
↑ Wind output
24m → ~5 m/s effective wind speed
{# Move 6 #}
⏰
Shift loads to daytime
Iron, freezer, water heater, grinder, pumps — run these 09:00–15:00 to use direct solar instead of battery.
↓ Battery need −30% ↓ Payback
Enabled: battery need reduces by ~30%
{# Move 7 #}
🔧
Reduce pipe head/distance
Every 10m of pipe friction head adds ~0.15 kW to pump requirements. Relocate pump closer or use wider pipes.
↓ Pump sizing
0% — current pipe layout
{# Move 8 — generator as backup only #}
🏭
Generator as backup only (< 20% hours)
Running generator 2 hrs/day instead of 8 hrs/day saves MWK 400K+ per month. Size solar to cover 80%+ of load.
↓ Payback −2 yrs ↓ Diesel spend
Disabled — generator running full time
