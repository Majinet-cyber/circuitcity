Series vs parallel explained — why it matters
BATTERIES IN SERIES

Voltage increases, capacity stays the same.
2× 12V 100Ah → 24V 100Ah bank.
Use when system voltage is higher than single battery voltage.
Example: Need 48V? Stack 4× 12V in series.

BATTERIES IN PARALLEL

Capacity increases, voltage stays the same.
2× 12V 100Ah → 12V 200Ah bank.
Use when you need more energy storage at same voltage.
Caution: Balance cells carefully. Max 4 strings recommended.

SERIES + PARALLEL (MIXED)

Both voltage and capacity increase.
4× 12V 100Ah (2S2P) → 24V 200Ah bank.
Series first to reach system voltage, then parallel for capacity.
Best approach for large banks. Label strings clearly.
