Sound Notifications

Premium Web Audio tones play when you record a sale, add stock, or collect a payment. Sounds are synthesized in-browser — no files, no lag. Enabled per device.

💰 Sale Complete

Premium ascending chime — plays after every successful sale

📦 Stock Added

Double-ping confirmation — plays when stock is received

✅ Payment Received

Warm two-note tone — plays when a payment is collected

📋 Credit Sale

Reserved rising tone — signals a credit/layby tab opened

Sounds are device-specific. Enabling here affects this browser only. The toggle in the top navigation bar also controls this setting.
