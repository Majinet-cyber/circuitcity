Bulk Add Members

How it works:
  1. Paste member data below (one per line)
  2. Supported formats: CSV or pipe-separated
  3. Members are created with status "Pending Payment"
  4. Add payments individually later as normal
  5. Duplicates are automatically detected and skipped
Format Examples:
CSV format:
John Doe, 0999123456, john@example.com
Jane Smith, 0888765432, jane@example.com
Bob Wilson, 0777654321

Pipe-separated format:
John Doe | 0999123456 | john@example.com
Jane Smith | 0888765432 | jane@example.com
Bob Wilson | 0777654321

Note: Name is required. Phone and email are optional but recommended.

Quick Tips
  • Excel/Google Sheets: Copy cells and paste directly (comma-separated)
  • Empty fields: Leave phone or email blank if unknown
  • Name format: Letters, spaces, hyphens, and apostrophes only
  • Phone format: At least 7 digits (separators like spaces/dashes are OK)
  • After creation: Members will appear in the members list with "Pending Payment" status
