EMAJINET MARKETPLACE
Merchant Participation Agreement

1. Parties

This Merchant Participation Agreement ("Agreement") is entered between:

  • Platform Provider: Emajinet / Circuit City Ltd., a registered technology company providing business management and marketplace services ("Emajinet").
  • Merchant: {{ business.name }}, operating on the Emajinet platform ("Merchant").

2. Subscription & Services

The Merchant has enrolled on an Emajinet subscription plan. This plan governs the level of marketplace access, feature limits, and support tier available to the Merchant. Plan details and pricing are as outlined in the Emajinet pricing schedule at the time of agreement.

3. Marketplace Terms

  • The Merchant may list products and services on the Emajinet Marketplace subject to these terms and the platform's listing guidelines.
  • All listings must be accurate, lawful, and represent goods or services the Merchant is authorised to sell. Fraudulent or misleading listings are strictly prohibited.
  • Emajinet reserves the right to review, reject, or remove any listing that violates platform policies or applicable laws.
  • The Merchant is solely responsible for fulfilling orders placed through the Marketplace and for the quality of goods/services provided.
  • Verified listings will receive a Verified Seller badge after satisfactory HQ review.

4. Payment Terms

Subscription fees are due on the billing date agreed at sign-up. Marketplace commission applies to sales completed through the Emajinet Marketplace checkout. Commission is deducted before seller net earnings are recorded. Emajinet will not process payouts for transactions flagged as fraudulent.

5. Merchant Verification

Emajinet may require the Merchant to provide business registration documents, national identification, or other verification materials. Unverified merchants may have limited listing visibility until verification is complete.

6. Suspension and Takedown

Emajinet may immediately suspend or terminate this Agreement and remove the Merchant's listings if the Merchant:

  • Provides false, misleading, or fraudulent information;
  • Violates consumer protection laws or platform policies;
  • Fails to fulfil orders or engages in deceptive practices;
  • Fails to maintain subscription payments for more than 30 days after due date;
  • Engages in behaviour that damages the reputation or security of the platform.

The Merchant may appeal a suspension by contacting HQ support within 14 days.

7. Confidentiality & Data

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of non-public information shared under this Agreement. Emajinet processes Merchant data in accordance with its Privacy Policy. The Merchant must not misuse access to customer data obtained through the platform.

8. General Provisions

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Malawi. Disputes shall be resolved by mutual negotiation; failing which, by mediation in accordance with applicable law.
