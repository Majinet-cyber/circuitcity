Standard Merchant Services Agreement

This is the standard contract used for all Emajinet merchants.

How to Use This Template

  1. Download the PDF template below
  2. Fill in the merchant's business details (highlighted sections)
  3. Send to the merchant via email for signature
  4. Once signed, upload it to the merchant's profile in the Contracts section
Download Contract Template (PDF) Preview Template

Template Contents

The standard contract includes the following sections:

  • Agreement Purpose
  • Services Provided
  • Subscription & Payment Terms
  • Data Ownership & Privacy
  • Merchant Responsibilities
  • Intellectual Property
  • Service Level & Uptime
  • Limitation of Liability
  • Indemnification
  • Termination
  • Confidentiality
  • Dispute Resolution
  • General Provisions
  • Signature Section

Important Notes

  • Legal Review: Have contracts reviewed by legal counsel before use
  • Customization: Some businesses may require customized terms (Enterprise tier)
  • Signatures: Digital signatures (DocuSign, HelloSign) or physical signatures are acceptable
  • Record Keeping: Always upload the signed contract to the merchant's profile
