How the TengaSale application queue works for sales reps.

How Claiming Works

Applications submitted by merchants are placed in a queue ordered by submission time. Press CLAIM NEXT to take the oldest unclaimed application. Once claimed, it becomes yours to review through all the checklist steps and reach a decision.

Cooldown Period

After claiming an application, you must wait {{ rule.cooldown_minutes }} minute{{ rule.cooldown_minutes|pluralize }} before claiming another. This prevents one rep claiming all applications at once.

Application Limit

You can have a maximum of {{ rule.max_active_applications }} active applications at any time. Complete a review before claiming more. Applications waiting for corrections for more than {{ rule.waiting_edit_grace_minutes }} minutes do not count toward this limit.

Home Page Polling

Your home page automatically checks the queue every {{ rule.polling_interval_seconds }} seconds to update the queue count and your claim button status. After {{ rule.inactive_pause_minutes }} minutes of inactivity, polling pauses to save battery.

Customer Edits

If corrections are sent back to the merchant, the application pauses. If the merchant does not resubmit within {{ rule.waiting_edit_grace_minutes }} minutes, it stops counting toward your active limit while you wait.

Approval Responsibility

Once you claim an application, you are responsible for reviewing it thoroughly and making a fair approval or rejection decision. Approvals create a binding TengaSale contract for the customer. Only approve applications that genuinely pass all checks.
