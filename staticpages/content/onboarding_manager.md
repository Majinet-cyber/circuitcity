# Manager Onboarding Guide

Welcome to **Emajinet** (Circuit City) – your all-in-one business management platform!

## What is Emajinet?

Emajinet is a powerful multi-tenant platform designed to help you manage your business efficiently. Whether you run a phone shop, pharmacy, liquor store, clothing boutique, or gym, Emajinet provides tailored tools to track inventory, sales, costs, and profits in real-time.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Logging In

1. Visit your Emajinet portal
2. Enter your username and password
3. After login, you'll be prompted to select your **Business** and **Location**

**Important:** Always ensure you've selected the correct business and location before performing any operations.

### 2. Understanding the Dashboard

Your dashboard is the heart of Emajinet. Here you'll see:

- **Today's Revenue & Profit**: Real-time financial metrics
- **Sales Count**: Number of transactions today
- **Top Products**: Your best-selling items
- **Payment Mix**: Cash vs Bank vs Mobile Money breakdown
- **Alerts**: Low stock, expiring items (pharmacy), warranty expirations (phones)

**Pro Tip:** Use the date range filters to analyze performance over different time periods (Today, Last 7 Days, This Month, Custom).

## Core Modules

### 📦 Inventory Management

**Purpose:** Track all your products and stock levels.

**Key Actions:**
- **Stock In**: Add new products to your inventory
- **View Inventory**: See all products with current stock levels
- **Batch Management** (Pharmacy): Track batches with expiry dates
- **Serial Numbers** (Phones): Track individual phones with IMEI numbers

**Best Practice:** Always record stock as soon as it arrives to maintain accurate inventory.

### 💰 Scan & Sell

**Purpose:** Quick sales interface for agents and cashiers.

**Features:**
- Barcode/QR code scanning
- Quick product search
- Multiple payment methods (Cash, Bank, Mobile Money)
- Instant commission calculation for agents

**Steps to Make a Sale:**
1. Select or scan product
2. Enter quantity
3. Choose payment method
4. Confirm sale
5. Print/send receipt (optional)

### 💳 Wallet & Transactions

**Purpose:** Track all financial transactions in one place.

**What's Tracked:**
- Sales revenue
- Agent commissions
- Business costs (rent, utilities, salaries, etc.)
- Withdrawals and deposits

**Important:** Record ALL costs to get accurate profit calculations!

### 📊 Costs Management

**Purpose:** Record all business expenses.

**Categories:**
- Rent
- Utilities (electricity, water, internet)
- Salaries & wages
- Marketing
- Supplies
- Other operational costs

**Why It Matters:** Your true profit = Revenue - Costs - Inventory Costs. Missing cost entries will give you inflated profit numbers!

### 🛍️ Layby (Installments)

**Purpose:** Manage customer installment purchases.

**Features:**
- Create layby agreements
- Track payments
- Automatic penalty calculations for late payments
- Payment reminders via WhatsApp

**Best Practice:** Always collect at least 30% deposit upfront.

### ⏰ Time Logs

**Purpose:** Track agent work hours and attendance.

**Actions:**
- Clock In/Out
- View timesheets
- Export attendance reports

**Manager Note:** Review time logs weekly to ensure accurate payroll.

### 📈 Reports

**Purpose:** Generate detailed business insights.

**Available Reports:**
- Sales by product
- Sales by agent
- Profit & Loss statements
- Stock valuation
- Commission summaries
- Trend analysis

**Pro Tip:** Export reports to PDF or Excel for record-keeping.

### 🎮 Simulator

**Purpose:** Plan and forecast your business performance.

**Use Cases:**
- Test different pricing strategies
- Calculate breakeven points
- Plan for growth scenarios
- Understand impact of volume changes

## Agent Management

### Assigning Roles

You can assign users different roles:
- **Owner**: Full access (you!)
- **Manager**: Most administrative functions
- **Agent**: Sales and basic inventory access
- **Viewer**: Read-only access for reports

### Commission Structure

Agents earn commissions on sales. Default rate is **12%** of the selling price.

**To Configure:**
1. Go to Sales → Commission Config
2. Set default commission rate
3. Optionally set product-specific rates

**Important:** Commissions are automatically calculated and added to agent wallets!

## Best Practices

### ✅ Daily Checklist

**Morning:**
- [ ] Clock in (if applicable)
- [ ] Review yesterday's summary
- [ ] Check low stock alerts
- [ ] Check expiring items (pharmacy) or warranties (phones)

- [ ] Record all sales immediately

- [ ] Review today's revenue and profit

### ✅ Monthly Tasks

- [ ] Generate monthly profit & loss report
- [ ] Review commission payouts

## Important Reminders

⚠️ **Data Security**
- Never share your password
- Always log out on shared devices
- Review audit logs regularly for unusual activity

⚠️ **Accuracy**
- Double-check quantities before confirming sales or stock-in
- Record costs immediately – don't rely on memory
- Reconcile your wallet transactions weekly

⚠️ **Communication**
- Keep your WhatsApp number updated for notifications
- Communicate with HQ about any technical issues
- Share feedback to help us improve!
