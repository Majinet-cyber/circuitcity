The Problem

Most merchants run blind.

If you carry inventory — whether in a phone shop, clothing store, liquor outlet, or hardware business — money is leaking from places you cannot see without the right system.

1.6%

Inventory shrinkage

The average retail business loses 1.6% of revenue to shrinkage — mostly from untracked stock and internal theft that goes undetected for months.

~60%

No system for sales tracking

Most small merchants rely on physical notebooks or memory. There is no record of who sold what, at what margin, or whether prices were applied correctly.

~70%

Cannot state their actual profit margin

Without cost-of-goods tracking and live margin calculations, most owners do not know whether their business is truly profitable until it is too late to act.

Core Platform

Operational control, end to end.

Six tightly integrated capabilities built for inventory-driven retail businesses.

Inventory Control

Track stock quantities, record goods received, and get alerts when items fall below reorder levels. Every unit accounted for.

Sales Tracking

Record every sale with the selling price, cost, and agent responsible. Get instant margin feedback per transaction.

Profit Visibility

See your gross profit, cost of goods sold, and margins at a glance — daily, weekly, or monthly. Know your real position at all times.

Staff Accountability

Assign roles and track performance per agent. See who's selling what, flag irregular activity, and maintain a full audit trail.

Smart Alerts

Get notified of low stock, below-cost sales, unusual movement patterns, and margin warnings before they become losses.

Reporting

Generate reports on sales performance, stock movement, and agent activity. Share clean summaries with your team or accountant.

Daily Reporting

Smart Recommendations

Emajinet reads your business data and surfaces the right insights at the right time — automatically.

Restock Prompts

Get prompted to reorder before you run out — based on sales velocity, lead time, and your configured minimum stock levels.

⚠ USB-C Charger — stock depletes in ~2 days. Stock up before the weekend.

Margin Warnings

Automatic flags on below-cost sales, thin margins, and pricing drift — before they erode your profit without you noticing.
