Our Mission

The operating system of small businesses. We give every shop, farm, and workshop an AI-powered backoffice that handles inventory, sales, and insights — so owners can focus on what they do best.

How It Works

Get started in minutes with our simple, powerful workflow

1

Sign Up & Set Up

Create your business in seconds. Add your locations and team members—works for any business type.

2

Add Your Records

Track inventory, seasons, or members depending on your business. Shops add products, farms add crops, gyms add members.

3

Run Daily Operations

Record sales, expenses, and payments. Track team performance with built-in wallet and layby features.

4

Get Recommendations

Receive smart prompts: restock alerts, margin warnings, renewal reminders, and season timing—so you know what to do next.

Emajinet adapts to your business type — shop, farm, gym, liquor, cement, and more.
