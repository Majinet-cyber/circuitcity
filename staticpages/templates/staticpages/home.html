The Problem

Most businesses operate blind.

They don't know real demand. They don't know their fastest-moving products. They can't state their actual margin. And money leaks from places they cannot see without the right system.

1.6%

Inventory shrinkage

Average retail loss from untracked stock and internal theft — invisible without a system.

~60%

No sales tracking system

Notebooks and memory replace records. No visibility on who sold what, at what price, or at what margin.

~70%

Margin blindness

Without cost tracking and live margin data, owners can't tell if they're profitable until it's too late.

—

Demand blindness

No system means no data on what's selling fast, what's slow, or what to restock — decisions made on gut, not fact.

The Reality

Only 2% of MSMEs in Malawi use the internet for business.

Among medium enterprises, just 9% use computerised record keeping. Without a system, owners lose track of inventory, sales, and profits — often for months before noticing.
Source: FinScope MSME Malawi 2019

Core Platform

Visibility. Intelligence. Control.

Six tightly integrated capabilities that eliminate blind spots across your entire operation.

Inventory Control

Track stock quantities, record goods received, and get alerts when items fall below reorder levels. Every unit accounted for.

Sales Tracking

Record every sale with the selling price, cost, and agent responsible. Get instant margin feedback per transaction.

Profit Visibility

See your gross profit, cost of goods sold, and margins at a glance — daily, weekly, or monthly. Know your real position at all times.

Staff Accountability

Assign roles and track performance per agent. See who's selling what, flag irregular activity, and maintain a full audit trail.

Smart Alerts

Get notified of low stock, below-cost sales, unusual movement patterns, and margin warnings before they become losses.

Reporting

Generate reports on sales performance, stock movement, and agent activity. Share clean summaries with your team or accountant.

Daily Reporting

Know your exact position at the end of every day.

Prediction beats reaction. Emajinet generates a clean daily summary of revenue, cost, gross profit, and margin — per product, per agent, and across every location.
