How Emajinet Business Credit Score works.

Built from consent-based business operating data inside Emajinet: sales, stock, cash flow, costs, obligations, payment discipline, and reporting completeness.

01
Business records operations

Merchants track sales, stock, recurring costs, payment activity, and operating history inside their business dashboard.

02
Score is calculated per business

The engine scores revenue consistency, profit trend, cash-flow stability, expense control, stock turnover, payment discipline, recurring obligations, and debt exposure.

03
Business gives consent

No business credit profile is shared without explicit business consent. Data is never exposed publicly or without authorisation.

04
Lender receives summary

Partners receive a structured business risk summary: score, label, financing readiness, recommended limit range, reasons, and improvement recommendations.

Data Privacy Commitment

  • No business credit profile is accessible without explicit consent from the business.
  • Lenders and partners cannot query raw data; they receive only aggregated, consent-authorised summaries.
  • All data access is logged in the Emajinet audit trail.
  • Emajinet does not sell credit data to third parties.
  • This system is designed to empower African SMEs, not exploit their data.
