🔒 Consent-based only 📊 Behaviour-driven scoring 🌍 Africa-first model 🏦 Lender-ready reports 🔐 No public data exposure
How Emajinet Credit Score works.

Built from actual layby repayment behavior inside Emajinet businesses — not credit bureau files.

01
Business records transactions

Merchants on Emajinet track every layby order, instalment, and repayment inside their business dashboard.

02
Score is calculated per customer

Our engine scores repayment speed, default behavior, debt exposure, purchase consistency, and relationship duration.

03
Customer gives consent

No score is shared without explicit consent from the customer. Data is never exposed publicly or without authorisation.

04
Lender receives risk summary

Partners receive a structured risk summary: score, label, recommendation (approve / limit / cash only / follow up).

🔒 Data Privacy Commitment

  • No customer score or business data is accessible without explicit consent from the business and the customer.
  • Lenders and partners cannot query raw data — only aggregated, consent-authorised summaries.
  • All data access is logged in the Emajinet audit trail.
  • Emajinet does not sell credit data to third parties.
  • This system is designed to empower African SMEs — not exploit their data.
