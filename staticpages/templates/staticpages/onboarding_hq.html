What is the HQ Portal?

The HQ portal provides superuser capabilities to manage the entire Emajinet platform. As HQ staff, you have visibility across all businesses, subscriptions, agents, and system-wide analytics.

Security Note

HQ access is restricted. Never share HQ credentials. All actions are logged in audit trails.

Business Management

Oversee All Merchants

View: /hq/businesses/

Key Information:

  • Business name, contact, and vertical
  • Status (Pending, Active, Suspended)
  • Subscription tier
  • Contract status (signed/not signed)
  • Activity metrics

Actions:

  1. Approve New Businesses: Review and activate pending applications
  2. View Business Details: Access full business profiles
  3. Suspend Businesses: Disable access for non-payment or violations
  4. Manage Contracts: Upload signed merchant agreements
Best Practice: Review new business applications within 24 hours.

Merchant Contracts

Contract Management Workflow

1. Download Template

  • Go to Contracts → Template
  • Download standard contract PDF
  • Customize if needed

2. Send to Merchant

  • Email contract to business owner
  • Request signature (digital or physical)

3. Upload Signed Contract

  • Click business in Contracts list
  • Upload signed PDF
  • Add notes (e.g., date, method)

4. Track Status

  • Signed - Contract on file
  • Not Signed - Pending action
Legal Note: Ensure all active paying customers have signed contracts on file.

Workflows & Processes

  1. Application Received: Review in /hq/businesses/
  2. Verification: Confirm business registration and contact info
  3. Contract: Send contract template for signature
  4. Approval: Activate business and assign trial subscription
  5. Onboarding Call: Schedule setup assistance
  6. Follow-Up: Check-in after 7 days
SLA: Complete onboarding within 48 hours of application.

  • 7 Days Before: Send renewal reminder email
  • 3 Days Before: Second reminder via email and WhatsApp
  • On Expiry: Grace period (3 days read-only)
  • After Grace: Suspend access, preserve data for 30 days

Important Reminders

Data Security

  • Never share HQ credentials
  • All access is logged in audit trails
  • Report security incidents immediately

Confidentiality

  • All merchant data is confidential
  • Do not share analytics externally
  • Follow NDA agreements
